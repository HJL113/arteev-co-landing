# Arteev Labs Inc. Static Homepage

This folder contains a simple static website for Arteev Labs Inc.

## Files

- `index.html` — main company homepage
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy page
- `terms.html` — terms of service page
- `styles.css` — shared styling

## Positioning

The site positions Arteev as a wellness pattern intelligence product/company, not a medical or healthcare provider.

Core compliance-safe language:
- General wellness awareness
- Habit reflection
- Personal pattern discovery
- Not medical advice
- Not diagnosis, treatment, cure, monitoring, or prevention of disease
- HealthKit access only with explicit user consent
- Subscription infrastructure may use RevenueCat

## Recommended Deployment

Fastest options:

1. Netlify
2. Vercel
3. Cloudflare Pages
4. Any basic static web hosting provider

## Domain Setup

After deploying the site, connect the domain `arteev.co` through your hosting provider's domain settings.

Typical DNS setup:
- Add an `A` record or `CNAME` record provided by your hosting platform.
- Add a `www` CNAME if you want `www.arteev.co` to work.
- Enable HTTPS/SSL in the hosting dashboard.

## Apple Developer Enrollment Note

For Apple Organization enrollment, make sure the public website shows:
- Legal company name: Arteev Labs Inc.
- Business description
- Contact email using the company domain
- Business address
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service

Important: This is not legal advice. Before app launch, especially with HealthKit and subscriptions, review the policy language with legal counsel or a privacy professional.
