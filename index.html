Our flagship product

Arteev — a voice-first wellness journal that notices your patterns for you.

People talk to Arteev for 20–60 seconds about their day. The app combines that voice log with passive health signals from the device and returns an immediate visual reflection. Over time, the longer someone uses it, the more accurately Arteev can describe their personal pattern. Arteev is a wellness journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Approach

Privacy by default. Audio is transcribed on-device when possible and discarded immediately after processing. Health data access is optional and routed through the operating system's consent flow. We do not sell user data, ever.

Wellness, not medicine. Arteev helps users reflect on personal patterns. It is not a diagnostic tool, not a substitute for professional care, and does not generate medical advice.

Korea first. Our launch market is Korea. The product is localized in Korean, and our company is registered in the Republic of Korea. International availability follows.
