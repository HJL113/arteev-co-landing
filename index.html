For organizations · Arteev for Work

The next generation of people analytics.
We call it Organizational Pattern Intelligence.

Most companies already hold vast amounts of employee data — and still can’t see the patterns that shape organizational health. Arteev answers the questions that matter, without ever exposing an individual:

  • Did stress rise after the reorganization?
  • Did return-to-office improve wellbeing — or erode it?
  • Is the organization drifting toward burnout?
  • Are sleep and energy recovering after a hard quarter?

Employees get the full personal Arteev app — private, theirs. Leaders see only anonymized, aggregate patterns — never an individual. Not by policy, by construction.

No annual-survey lag, no gut-feel decisions: leaders watch patterns move as the organization changes, without breaking employee trust. Not more data — the patterns already in the data you have.

“Arteev doesn’t monitor people. We help organizations understand their own patterns.”

Aggregate only

Every number a leader sees is a group-level rollup. There is no individual view to request, grant, or leak.

Anonymous by construction

Metrics render only above a strict minimum group size. Words, recordings, and personal logs never leave the individual’s private space.

Employees own their data

Joining is explicit and revocable. Leaving the company ends the seat — never the person’s data.

Approach

Privacy by default. Audio is transcribed on-device when possible and discarded immediately after processing. Health data access is optional and routed through the operating system's consent flow. We do not sell user data, ever.

Wellness, not medicine. Arteev helps users reflect on personal patterns. It is not a diagnostic tool, not a substitute for professional care, and does not generate medical advice.

Patterns, not surveillance. For organizations, Arteev reports anonymized, aggregate patterns over time — associations, not diagnoses or causal claims. No employer can see an individual, and no individual-level view exists to be granted.
